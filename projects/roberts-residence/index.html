Landscape architecture

The Roberts Residence

Fairhope, Alabama · Landscape plan · 2022

Landscape architect · Paul Fontenot

Pools · Gardens · Courtyards · Estate Entrances

A hand-rendered landscape plan for an estate on Mobile Bay along Scenic Highway 98, drawn by GDSI landscape architect Paul Fontenot. The design sets a formal motor court and parterre garden against a bayfront pool, spa and pool house.

  • Landscape Architecture & Design
  • Planting Design
  • Pool & Spa Design
  • Hardscape Design
Design
Garden Design Solutions, Inc.
Landscape architect
Paul Fontenot, GDSI

The vision

A symmetrical, axial plan that gathers arrival, garden and waterfront into one calm, formal sequence from the highway to the bay.

Materials & planting
Plant palette
Eagleston Holly, Blue Hydrangea, Japanese Anise, Variegated Liriope, Loropetalum 'Platinum Beauty', Dwarf Sasanqua 'White', Boxwood, Asian Jasmine, Gardenia, Italian Cypress, Holly Fern, Indica Azalea, Liriope 'Emerald Goddess', Crape Myrtle, Hydrangea 'Limelight', Split-leaf Philodendron, Dwarf Ruellia
Hardscape materials
Formal motor court, Pool and spa, Pool house, Parterre garden, Brick walks, Retaining walls, Waterfront frontage (Mobile Bay)
