Landscape architecture

Tay's Memorial Garden

Mobile, Alabama · Landscape plan · 2022

Landscape architect · Paul Fontenot

Gardens · Courtyards

A hand-rendered plan for a formal memorial garden in Mobile, drawn by GDSI landscape architect Paul Fontenot. The garden is organized on a cross axis around a central fountain, enclosed by clipped hedges and brick walks between Old Shell Road and Tuthill Lane.

Design
Garden Design Solutions, Inc.
Landscape architect

The vision

A quiet, symmetrical memorial garden — green rooms gathered around a central fountain and a wall fountain, framed for stillness.

Materials & planting
Plant palette
Asian Jasmine, Loropetalum, Camellia Japonica, Chinese Privet (tree form), Indica Azalea 'Gerbing', Dwarf Boxwood
Hardscape materials
Central fountain, Wall fountain, Cross-axis brick walks, Brick borders, Stucco columns, Low fence with brick columns, Formal lawns
